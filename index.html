    Capital Wars

    18×18 battlefield, 9 territories. Hold 6 capitals on your own, or wipe out every enemy dot.

    Computer level

    Two players take turns on the same device. Not rated.

    Map
    Battlefield skin

    Rules

    Victory

    • Control 6 capitals on your own
    • OR eliminate all of your opponent's dots

    Board

    18×18 grid split into 9 territories (6×6). The gold 2×2 block in the middle of each territory is a capital. You control it if one of your dots is inside it or right next to it (the dashed 4×4 area). If both players have a dot there, it is contested: both get its reinforcement, but it doesn't count toward the 6.

    Setup

    Black starts inside the top-left capital on its outer corner. White starts inside the bottom-right capital on its inner corner, one step closer to the centre, to make up for moving second.

    Turn

    • 1. Reinforce: +1 dot per capital you control (contested ones count), max 3. Place each next to one of your dots (diagonals count) that is connected to a capital.
    • 2. Action ×3: move or attack. The same dot may act more than once.
    • Move: 1 square in any of 8 directions. You can't slip diagonally between 2 enemy dots (or mountains).

    Combat

    • Attacker rolls d20, defender rolls d12. The higher total wins and the losing dot is removed. The winner gains 1 capture piece.
    • If the attacker wins, the attacking dot moves in. On a tie nothing happens and the action is used up.
    • Fortification: a defender inside a capital, attacked from outside it, gets +1 for each of its own dots inside that capital. The capital's walls grow taller as more dots wait inside.
    • Spending: after the roll, pay 2 capture pieces to force your opponent to reroll.

    Surrounding

    A dot whose 4 orthogonal neighbours, or whose 4 diagonal neighbours, are all enemy dots is captured immediately. Board edges and mountains count as walls, but at least 2 enemy dots are needed.

    Terrain (Terrain map only)

    • River: a dot standing in a river gets −1 on its roll, whether attacking or defending.
    • Forest: a dot defending in a forest gets +1.
    • Hill: a dot attacking from a hill gets +1.
    • Mountain: impassable. Nobody can move or deploy there.

    Terrain maps are generated mirrored (rotated 180°), so both sides get the same ground.

    Who goes first

    Black moves first. White starts deeper in its capital and automatically wins its first battle.

    Rating (Elo)

    Everyone starts at 1000. Online games and games against the computer change your rating. Beating a stronger player earns more points, and losing to a weaker one costs more. The lobby marks fair matches (within 200 points).

    Interpretations
    · Capitals are 2×2. "Adjacent" includes diagonals.
    · If the defender wins, the attacking dot is removed.
    · White's first-battle bonus applies to the first battle White is in, whether attacking or defending.
    · Only the side that is losing (or tied) can spend captures to force a reroll.
